# Essential Spoken English Rules & Common Mistakes

## Rule 1: Subject-Verb Agreement
Singular subjects take singular verbs, plural subjects take plural verbs.
- Incorrect: "He go to work every day."
- Correct: "He goes to work every day."
- Incorrect: "They was happy."
- Correct: "They were happy."

## Rule 2: Prepositions of Time (In, On, At)
- **At**: Specific times (at 5 PM, at midnight, at noon).
- **On**: Days and dates (on Monday, on May 5th, on my birthday).
- **In**: Long periods, months, years, seasons (in May, in 2026, in summer, in the morning).

## Rule 3: Articles (A, An, The)
- Use **A** before consonant sounds: "a book", "a university" (starts with 'y' sound).
- Use **An** before vowel sounds: "an apple", "an hour" (silent 'h').
- Use **The** for specific known items: "the teacher", "the sun".

## Rule 4: Using Articles with Uncountable Nouns
Do not use 'a' or 'an' with uncountable nouns (information, advice, furniture, luggage).
- Incorrect: "Give me an advice."
- Correct: "Give me some advice" or "Give me a piece of advice."
