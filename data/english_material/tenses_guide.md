# Overview of English Tenses

## 1. Present Tenses
- **Present Simple**: Habits, general truths, daily routines.
  - Structure: Subject + Verb(s/es)
  - Example: "I practice English every day."
- **Present Continuous**: Actions happening right now at the moment of speaking.
  - Structure: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing
  - Example: "I am learning English with SpeakWise."
- **Present Perfect**: Past actions with relevance to the present.
  - Structure: Subject + have/has + V3
  - Example: "I have improved my vocabulary."

## 2. Past Tenses
- **Past Simple**: Completed actions at a specific time in the past.
  - Structure: Subject + Past Verb (V2)
  - Example: "I spoke to my friend yesterday."
- **Past Continuous**: Action in progress at a specific time in the past.
  - Structure: Subject + was/were + Verb-ing
  - Example: "I was listening to music when you called."

## 3. Future Tenses
- **Future Simple (will)**: Instant decisions, predictions, promises.
  - Structure: Subject + will + Base Verb
  - Example: "I will call you tonight."
- **Be going to**: Planned future actions or intentions.
  - Structure: Subject + am/is/are + going to + Base Verb
  - Example: "I am going to study English tomorrow."
